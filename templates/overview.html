ANOMALIES DETECTED

What the detector flagged

An energy anomaly is an hour where a building's measured load differed from the forecast by 25% or more. The measure column shows that gap: −30% means the building drew 30% less than expected. Severity is HIGH from 50% upward, ELEVATED below it. A peak-demand risk is a different thing entirely: the campus total climbing toward the 850 kW grid limit, and its measure is the share of that limit reached.
